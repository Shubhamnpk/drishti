# Drishti v1.3.0 - More ways to explore Nepal's public data

This release expands Drishti's market, procurement, government agency, news, and disaster experiences, with clearer maps, search, and page metadata across the site.

## What's included

- **Government agencies:** a searchable directory, detailed agency profiles, and an API-backed catalogue that can be explored by organization and function.
- **NEPSE market tools:** dedicated broker profiles and directory, a floor-sheet view, and improvements to the market board and company detail experience.
- **Procurement:** a live PPMO e-GP feed with tender search, filtering, sorting, publisher rankings, agency pages, and tender detail views for schedules, amendments, and documents. Missing source document links now degrade gracefully.
- **Government updates:** a news and updates feed with individual detail pages, translation support, share previews, and search-engine metadata.
- **Maps and disasters:** more useful 2D/3D map controls and indicator layers, improved earthquake history and details, and an earthquake section with map links on the disaster page.
- **Places and projects:** refinements to project discovery, search, province and district profiles, census visualizations, and responsive layouts.
- **Discoverability:** improved page metadata, sitemap coverage, structured data, and a custom interactive not-found page.
- **Delivery and data handling:** route caching and deployment workflow improvements for more consistent updates.

## Sources and limitations

Live data remains subject to the availability and completeness of its original publisher. Procurement document links and amounts appear only when the e-GP source provides them; archived OCDS records remain separate from live notices. Earthquake records are supplied by USGS, while official disaster incident, loss, relief, and budget records are supplied by BIPAD/NDRRMA.
